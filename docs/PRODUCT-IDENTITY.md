# SqAId product identities

All product marks are scalable SVGs in `public/assets/product-logos/`. Each has a light-background version and a `-dark.svg` version. `ProductLogo` selects the correct asset for the site theme.

- **ARGUS:** Preserves the original triangular network supplied in the ARGUS project. The connected A represents the unified platform and connected entities. Dark mode changes the navy structure to pale periwinkle for contrast; colored nodes remain intact.
- **ABACUS:** Recreates the attached rail-and-bead motif in the website’s amber palette. Offset beads evoke complementary screening routes and comparison of candidate evidence.
- **SqAId Brain:** Connected neural branches converge on a central hub, representing coordinated agents and intelligence across products. Blue and cyan identity.
- **UDM:** A warehouse roof surrounds connected data layers, expressing the enterprise warehouse and shared data foundation powering ARGUS. Cobalt identity.
- **SqAId KYC:** An identity silhouette within a shield with a check represents customer due diligence and verified identity. Teal identity.

The marks appear in desktop and mobile product navigation, product page headings, product cards, the platform architecture and the footer. Operational interface icons remain separate from product identities.
